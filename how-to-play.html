How to Play

I.

Lobby & Roles

3–6 players enter the campus after curfew. Once the match begins, 1–2 players are randomly chosen in secret as Hunters while the rest become Survivors.

II.

Survivors' Goal

Explore the darkened quad, library, and halls to complete all assigned rituals quietly, then escape before time runs out or everyone is caught.

III.

Hunters' Goal

Patrol the campus shadows, track down lingering footsteps, and eliminate every Survivor before the final ritual is completed.

IV.

Survival Tips

Listen closely for spatial footsteps and heartbeat cues, slip into deep architectural shadows to hide, and stick together or split tasks strategically.
